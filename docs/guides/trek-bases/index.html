Trek-base stays: Munsiyari, Auli and Chopta

A trek is only as good as its base: a bed before the trail, a bed after it, and a host who knows the route. These are the three bases Nesto covers today — and an honest account of how much coverage that is.

By Nesto Stays · 1 November 2026 · Stay counts as of 1 November 2026

Munsiyari and Chaukori — the far east

Munsiyari, at around 2,200 metres, is a far-flung hill town at the edge of the high Himalaya and the base for treks to the Milam Glacier, one of the Kumaon Himalaya's great glacier routes. Its viewpoints look out to the Panchachuli peaks. Nearby Chaukori is a sleepier village of tea gardens, known for sunrise over those same peaks. Come here for serious walking and long, quiet evenings — and confirm trek conditions with your host, who will know the season better than any booking page.

Auli and Joshimath — snow and meadows

Auli, above Joshimath at around 2,800 metres, is Uttarakhand's best-known skiing destination, with wide views of Nanda Devi. Its ropeway runs about 4 km from Joshimath, one of the longest cable cars in India. December to February is skiing season; March to June is meadows and views. Joshimath itself is the gateway town — to Badrinath in one direction, to Auli in the other — and home to one of the four maths established by Adi Shankaracharya.

Chopta and Tungnath — the short, high trek

Chopta, at around 2,700 metres, is a meadow village nicknamed Mini Switzerland and the base for the trek to Tungnath, the highest Shiva temple in the world. It is the most accessible of the three bases in this guide — a short trek with a very high payoff. One caution worth stating plainly: the Tungnath temple itself is closed in winter, roughly November to April, so check locally before planning a temple visit. The meadows are still there in winter; the temple is not.

What Nesto actually has here

As of 1 November 2026, Nesto's Trek bases collection holds 7 stays: 3 in Munsiyari / Chaukori, 2 in Auli / Joshimath and 2 in Chopta / Tungnath. That is small, and we would rather say so than imply otherwise. These valleys are where Nesto is growing next — hosts here are being reached through local creators — and the collection grows as they claim their listings. If a base you want is not listed yet, message Nesto and we will try to reach a host there; that is how the map has grown so far.
