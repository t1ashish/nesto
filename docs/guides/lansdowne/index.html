Lansdowne homestays: a quiet cantonment town, booked direct

Pine forests, colonial-era calm and far fewer crowds than the famous hill stations. If Mussoorie feels too busy, Lansdowne is the quieter answer, and its homestays are the way to stay there properly.

By Nesto Stays · 1 November 2026 · Stay counts as of 1 November 2026

What Lansdowne is

Lansdowne is a cantonment town at around 1,700 metres, named after Lord Lansdowne, the Viceroy of India in 1888–1894, and it remains an army town today. That history is the reason it feels different: pine forests and unhurried streets instead of mall-road crowds. It is the kind of place where a homestay makes more sense than a hotel, because the town itself is residential and quiet: you stay in a home, in a town of homes.

What to see

  • Bhulla Tal: the town's small lake, good for an easy morning walk.
  • Garhwal Rifles Museum: the regiment's history, in the town the regiment calls home.
  • Tip-in-Top viewpoint: the classic Lansdowne viewpoint over the hills.
  • St. Mary's Church: a colonial-era church in the cantonment.

Getting there

The nearest airport is Jolly Grant, Dehradun, and the nearest railhead is Kotdwar. From Kotdwar the road climbs into the hills to Lansdowne. Ask your host about the last stretch before you travel. Hosts know the current road and the best arrival time, and that is exactly the kind of question worth asking on WhatsApp before you book.
