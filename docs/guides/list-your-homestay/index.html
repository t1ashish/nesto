Own a homestay? List it free on Nesto

Your homestay may already be listed. Claiming it costs nothing, and it puts you in direct touch with guests: no commission, no middleman, no waiting for a booking site to pass on your own bookings.

By Nesto Stays · 1 November 2026

What Nesto is

Nesto is a host-first directory of independent Uttarakhand homestays. Guests browse stays, then message the host directly on WhatsApp before booking, and they pay the host directly, by UPI or cash. Nesto takes zero commission on room revenue, ever. The first 100 hosts list free, and a future Host Pro tier (₹3,000 a year) will add tools for hosts who want them. As of 1 November 2026, Nesto lists 158 stays.

How claiming works

  1. Find your listing. Search the portal for your homestay's name or town. Many listings come from the state tourism registry, so yours may already be there.
  2. Tap "Claim now". It opens WhatsApp with a claim message ready to send, including your listing's claim code.
  3. Send it from your listed number. The message itself is the proof: a claim sent from the phone number already associated with the homestay shows the listing is genuinely yours.
  4. Correct and complete. Tell Nesto what to fix (price, photos, rooms, meals) and answer three questions that earn your listing its badges: is it run by a woman host, is it open in winter, and does it serve home-cooked meals?
  5. Get verified. Once the number matches and details check out, your listing becomes Nesto Verified, and verified hosts get featured in these guides.

What you keep

Everything a booking site normally takes: the 15–20% commission, the guest relationship, and the repeat booking. On Nesto, the guest who messages you is your guest. The next time they come, they message you directly. That is the whole business case in one line: the room revenue stays with the host, and the guest relationship stays with the host too.
