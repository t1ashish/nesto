Uttarakhand homestay subsidy and loans: what hosts can actually claim

Building a room or repairing one? Uttarakhand pays part of the cost for registered homestays, and banks lend against the rest. This page explains the schemes that exist, the terms as verified on 1 November 2026, and the honest limits. Nesto guides, it never lends and never promises approval.

By Nesto Stays · 1 November 2026

The main scheme: Deen Dayal homestay subsidy

The Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grah Awas (Homestay) Vikas Yojana is the state scheme for homestays, run by the Uttarakhand Tourism Development Board (UTDB). It pays a capital subsidy on the project cost, an interest subsidy on a bank loan, and SGST reimbursement on homestay income. It covers new homestays and the renovation or expansion of existing ones.

  • Capital subsidy: up to 50% of the project cost in hilly areas, capped at ₹15 lakh; up to 25% in plain areas, capped at ₹7.5 lakh.
  • Interest subsidy: 50% of the interest on a bank loan, up to ₹1.5 lakh a year in hilly areas and ₹1 lakh a year in plain areas, for five years.
  • SGST reimbursement: 100% of the state GST on homestay income, reported as five years in 2026 coverage.
  • Your contribution: 12.5% of the project cost, paid when the loan is taken.

Who qualifies. Permanent residents of Uttarakhand. A state cabinet decision in early 2026 restricted scheme benefits to state residents, after concerns that outsiders were buying property for commercial use without contributing to the village. The home must be owner occupied, with 1 to 6 rooms, registered with UTDB, and a family member must stay at the homestay and manage it. Areas under municipal corporations are excluded.

Documents usually asked for. Aadhaar, permanent residence proof, land or building documents, and a project estimate. Apply through the Mukhyamantri Swarojgar Yojana portal, and confirm the current terms with UTDB or your District Tourism Office before you apply.

Registration with UTDB is mandatory for homestays under the 2026 Tourism and Travel Business Registration Rules. Separately, the state runs its own booking portal, UttaraStays, where homestay owners can list at no platform fee.

Read this before you rely on the numbers. Older government pages, including the District Pauri Garhwal tourism page, still show the earlier terms: 33% up to ₹10 lakh in the hills and three years of SGST relief. Reporting through 2026 consistently shows the higher terms above. Some 2026 articles also describe the interest cap as a five year total rather than a yearly cap, while the district government page describes it per year. The official pages have not all caught up, so the District Tourism Office has the final word at application time.

Trekking villages: a smaller grant, simpler rules

The Trekking Traction Centre Homestay Scheme (2020) targets villages within 2 km of notified trekking routes, where conventional hotels are unfeasible. 66 villages were notified in phases 1 to 3.

  • ₹60,000 per new room with an attached toilet, up to 6 rooms.
  • ₹25,000 per room for decoration or repair of existing rooms.
  • Selection by a committee chaired by the District Magistrate, with money transferred directly to the beneficiary bank account after inspection.

Check whether your village is notified and whether a new phase is open. This grant suits remote trek route villages, not every homestay.

The wider tourism loan scheme

The Veer Chandra Singh Garhwali Tourism Swarojgar Yojana gives a bank loan plus a subsidy for tourism businesses, and homestays are explicitly included, alongside hotels, vehicles, adventure tourism and more. The subsidy is 33% in hilly areas and 25% in plain areas, with different caps for different items and separate terms for vehicles. Applications go through the same Mukhyamantri Swarojgar Yojana portal, and the nodal agency is the Tourism Department. If the homestay scheme does not fit you, this is the next door to try.

A bank loan without any subsidy

Hosts who do not qualify for a subsidy, for example because they are not permanent residents or their property sits in a municipal area, can still approach a bank for a regular loan. Take your homestay registration, a project estimate, and your income records. Central options such as MUDRA and PMEGP exist for small businesses generally, but they are not homestay specific, so ask your bank what fits your case. Nesto has no lender tie-up today, so this page explains the schemes you can apply for directly. If Nesto ties up with lenders later, verified hosts will be first in line.

Size the loan against net income, not gross

A model homestay in Nesto's 1 November 2026 economics analysis, 3 rooms at ₹2,000 a night and 25% occupancy, grosses about ₹5.48 lakh a year and nets about ₹2.19 lakh. That net figure is before loan repayments and before unpaid family labour. A loan payment comes out of the ₹2.19 lakh, not the ₹5.48 lakh, so borrowing against the gross figure is how hosts over-borrow. Work the repayment into the net figure first.
