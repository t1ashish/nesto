The Quiet Hills: Pauri homestays without the crowds

Most Uttarakhand itineraries run the same corridor: Haridwar, Rishikesh, Nainital, Mussoorie. This one doesn't. It runs through Pauri Garhwal, the district the big platforms barely cover, and it is where Nesto is strongest.

By Nesto Stays · 1 November 2026 · Stay counts as of 1 November 2026

The circuit

Base yourself in Lansdowne or Pauri town and take slow days from there. Lansdowne gives you the cantonment calm: pine forests, Bhulla Tal lake, the Garhwal Rifles Museum and the Tip-in-Top viewpoint. Pauri town, the headquarters of Pauri Garhwal district at around 1,700 metres, gives you wide Himalayan views and temples like Kandoliya and Nagdev, well off the usual tourist circuit. Between them sits Khirsu, a small village in Pauri district that travellers keep asking about precisely because it is quiet.

Why this circuit works as homestays

These are residential hill towns, not resort towns. The best homestays here are family homes, and the best way to book them is to talk to the host first, about the room, the food, the road and the weather. That conversation is Nesto's whole model: guests message hosts directly on WhatsApp before paying, and hosts keep every rupee of the room price, because Nesto takes zero commission on stays.

What Nesto actually has here

As of 1 November 2026, Nesto's Quiet Hills collection holds 132 stays: 130 in Pauri Garhwal and 2 in the Lansdowne region. That is the deepest coverage Nesto has anywhere, and it is also the honest shape of it. Khirsu itself has just one Nesto listing today; the circuit works because the district around it is covered, not because every village is. As hosts in these towns claim their listings, the map fills in village by village.
