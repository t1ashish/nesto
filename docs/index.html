Our story

Nesto began with a simple frustration: the best hill homestays in Uttarakhand were invisible — buried under hotels on big booking sites that took a fifth of the host's income and wouldn't let you talk to the person you'd actually stay with. So we built the directory we wished existed: one where the host is the hero, the price is the host's price, and the booking starts with a conversation.

Our vision

A thousand Uttarakhand hosts earning directly from guests who chose them — not an algorithm. No commissions on stays, ever. Hosts own the guest relationship from booking one, and travelers book people, not inventory.

Become a host

Refer a host

Partner with us
