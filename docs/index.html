Our Story

Most homestays in Uttarakhand are found through big booking platforms that take 15–20% of every stay — and the host never meets the guest until check-in. Nesto started from a simple idea: the host is the stay. We list homestays the way they actually are — starting with 28 homes we documented by hand, then adding the state tourism registry district by district, Pauri Garhwal first — so a guest can talk to the host on WhatsApp before booking, and the host keeps every rupee of the room price.

n

Our Vision

Our vision is simple: every independent homestay in Uttarakhand in one place, with a host you can reach before you pay. No commission on the room, ever. Every listing honest about what its host has — and has not — confirmed, until verification becomes the norm, not the exception. If we do that right, booking a hill homestay will always start with a conversation, not a commission.
